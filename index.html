Thaburi ya 100 / Psalm 100

Anirirai Jehova, inyui mabururi o mothe, tungatagirai Jehova mukenete thii mbere yake mukiinaga. Menyai ati Jehova niwe Ngai, niwe watuumbire, na ithui turi ake, turi andu ake, na turi mburi cia ruuru rwake. Tonyerai ihingo ciake mukimugathaga, mutonyere nyumba ciake thiini, mumugathe, na ningi mukumie Ritwa riake. Ni undu Jehova ni mwega, na tha ciake ni cia tene na tene, na ni wa kwihokwo ni njiaro ciothe. Ithe witu arogocwo na Muriu, o na Roho Mutheru. O ta uria gwatarii kiambiriria, o na uria gutarii riu, na uria gugatuika hingo ciothe, tene na tene. Amen.

Shout for joy to the Lord, all the earth. Worship the Lord with gladness; come before him with joyful songs. Know that the Lord is God. It is he who made us, and we are his; we are his people, the sheep of his pasture. Enter his gates with thanksgiving and his courts with praise; give thanks to him and praise his name. For the Lord is good and his love endures forever; his faithfulness continues through all generations. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit; as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

Witikio wa Atumwo / Apostles' Creed

Ni njitikitie Ngai Ithe Mwene hinya woothe, Mumbi wa Iguru na thi, na ngetikia Jesu Kristo Muru wake na Mumwe, Mwathani witu waciarirwo ni Muiritu wetagwo Mariamu, wagiire ihu ni undu wa Roho Mutheru, akinyarirwo hindi ya Pointio Pilato, akiambwo muti iguru, agikua agithikwo, agiikuruka kwa ngoma. Muthenya wa gatatu akiriuka, akiuma kuri akuu, akihaica iguru, na riu aikaire guoko-ini kwa urio kwa Ngai Mwene Hinya wothe, ni akoima kuo, oke guku aciriithie aria me muoyo na aria makuite. Na ningi ni njitikitie Roho Mutheru, naguo kanitha Mutheru wa kundu guothe, naguo uiguano wa andu aria athem, nakuo kuoherwo kwa mehia, nakuo kuriuka kwa mwiri, naguo muoyo utagathira. Amen.

I believe in God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth. I believe in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried; he descended to the dead. On the third day he rose again; he ascended into heaven, he is seated at the right hand of the Father, and he will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting. Amen.

Witikio wa Ngwataniro Theru / Nicene Creed

Ni njitikitie Ngai Ithe Mwene hinya woothe, mumbi wa Iguru na thi, na wa indo ciothe cionekaga na iria itonekaga, na ngetikia Mwathani umwe Jesu Kristo, Muru wa Ngai uria waciarirwo niwe na mumwe, waciarirwo ni Ithe mbere ya mindi na mindi. Ngai umiite Ngai Utheri uumite Utheri Ngai wa ma uumite Ngai wa ma uria waciarirwo na ti kuumbwo ombirwo ningi muthemba wake na wa ithe ni muthemba omwe kuna. Nacio indo ciothe ciombirwo niwe uria waikurukire akiuma Iguru ni undu witu andu a thini getha tuhonokio akigia na mwiri, agiciarwo ni Muiritu wetagwo Mariamu na undu wa hinya wa Roho Mutheru agituika Mundu na ningi akiambwo muti iguru ni undu witu hindi ya Pointio Pilato akinyarirwo agithikwo muthenya wa gatatu akiriuka o ta uria kwandikitwo maandiko-ini Akihaica Iguru na riu aikaire guoko-ini kwa urio kwa Ngai ithe witu nake ni agacoka na unene acirithie aria me muoyo ona aria makuite naguo uthamaki wake ndugathira. Na ningi ni njitikitie Roho Mutheru Mwathani witu na muheani muoyo uumite kuri Ithe witu na uria hamwe na ithe witu uturagirio ndu na akagocwo/na uria waaragia na tunua twa Anabii na ngetikia kanitha umwe uria wi kundu guothe na ni umwe na uria wa atumwo na ngetikia ati ni kuri ubatithio o umwe wa kweheria mehia na ningi ni njetereire kuriuka kwa akuu/na muoyo wa hindi iria igoka. Amen.

Thaburi 95 / Psalm 95

Ukani tuinire Jehova, tukenete, twanirire ihiga ria gutuhonokia. Ni tuthii turugame mbere ya Jehova, tumugathe. Tumwanirire na thaburi tukenete. Ni undu Jehova ni we Ngai munene na niwe muthamaki munene gukira Ngai ciothe cianda ciothe ii moko-ini make o naguo hinya wa irima ui wake iria rinene ni riake na niwe wariumbire moko make nimo mombire thi ino. Ukai tumuhoe, tumuinamirire na tuturitie ndu Jehova mutuumbi. Ni undu niwe Ngai witu na ithui turi mugambo wake tigai kumia ngoro cianyu ota uria mwekire kuu meriba ona kuu Masa weru-ini. Hindi iria maithe manyu mangeririe ningi makinduiria na makiona wira wakwa. Ngiigua uuru ni undu wa ruciaro ruru miaka mirongo ina ngikiuga Aya ni andu mahitagia ngoro-ini ciao na matiui njira ciakwa. Ni undu ucio ngihita ndakariite ngiuga matigatonya kihuruko-ini giakwa. Ithe witu arogocwo ona Muriu! O na Roho Mutheru. Ota uria gwatarii, kiambiriria/na uria gutarii riu na uria gugatuika/hingo ciothe! Tene na tene Amen.

Gloria

Kugoocwo kurogia kwi Ngai o Iguru, nakuo thi kugie na thayu, andu nao maiguane, ni tugukugooca, ningi ni tugukugatha, na ni tuguguturiria ndu, ni tugugukumia, ni tugukwira athanda ni undu wa mwago waku munene, Wee Mwathani Ngai, Muthamaki wa Iguru, Ngai Ithe Mwene hinya wothe, Mwathani witu Jesu Kristo, Mwana uria waciarirwo wa Mumwe, Wee Mwathani Ngai, Gaturume ka Ngai, Muru wa Ithe Witu, Wee weheragia mehia ma andu a thi -ino, tuiguire tha+. Wee weheragia mehia ma andu a thi-ino, itikira ihoya ritu+. Wee uiakaraga guoko-ini kwa urio kwa Ngai ithe witu, tuiguire tha+ ni undu wee nowe mutheru, wee nowe Mwathani witu wiki, wee Kristo hamwe na Roho Mutheru, wi Iguru Muno muno unene-ini wa Ngai Ithe Witu. Amen.

AIPCA Tri-Bar Cross

Top Bar: Inscribed with “INRI” (Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews).

Middle Bar: Horizontal beam where Christ’s hands were nailed.

Slanted Bottom Bar: Footrest tilted as Jesus moved in pain when pierced by the spear.

Three Beams: Symbolizes the Holy Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit).

Seven Points: Represents Christ's seven final statements on the Cross.

Church History

Founded in the 1920s-1930s during the Kikuyu independent movement. Banned during the Mau Mau freedom struggle in 1952, and officially re-established post-independence, taking the name AIPCA in 1970.
